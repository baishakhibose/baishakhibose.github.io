People

Dr. Baishakhi Bose

Principal Investigator

Dr. Baishakhi Bose is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil and Construction Engineering and Management at The University of Texas at Tyler. Her research integrates sustainable materials, resilient infrastructure, and engineering innovation, with an emphasis on low-carbon and circular construction materials, material–structure relationships, and systems-level approaches to improving the performance and sustainability of the built environment.
